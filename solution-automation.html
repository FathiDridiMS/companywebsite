Process Automation

Eliminate manual work.

We analyse business processes, simplify them, automate the repetitive activities and integrate the systems your employees already use.

What we do

Automating a broken process just makes it fail faster. We start by understanding what the process is actually for, remove the steps that exist only for historical reasons, and then automate what remains.

The result runs inside the tools people already have — Teams, Outlook, SharePoint — rather than adding another system to learn.

Where the process has to reach an on-premises database, an ERP or a legacy line-of-business application, we connect through premium connectors and the on-premises data gateway rather than forcing a migration first.

Where a step requires judgement over unstructured content — reading a document, classifying a request, summarising a case — we add an AI layer rather than leaving a human to do clerical work inside an otherwise automated flow.
