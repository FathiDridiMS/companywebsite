Telecommunications · Sovereign on-premises AI

On-premises AI platform for contract lifecycle management

ClientThe incumbent telecom operator in its market
FunctionLegal Business Partner
ScopeB2B, B2C and wholesale contract portfolios
DeploymentFully on-premises, local AI farm
Context

The operator manages a large and growing portfolio of contracts and amendments across consumer, business and wholesale perimeters, with a strong need for centralisation and standardisation. An enterprise document management platform was already in place, covering centralisation, security, traceability, classification, search and secure archival. In parallel, the operator was structuring a local AI farm to address sensitive scenarios such as assisted contract analysis while keeping processing and data in a controlled environment.

Challenge

Contract drafting, review and compliance checking were slow and inconsistent across a large legal function. AI could accelerate this work substantially — but contracts, regulatory documents and legal content are precisely the material that cannot be sent to public cloud AI services. Data sovereignty was a hard constraint, not a preference.

Solution
  • An AI layer built on top of the existing document repository rather than replacing it — capitalising on the in-place centralisation, versioning, security, search and archival
  • Ingestion connectors reading contracts, amendments, internal reference material and regulatory corpora from the existing repository
  • OCR, normalisation, segmentation and metadata extraction pipeline feeding a vector database
  • Local LLM serving on the operator's own GPU infrastructure, orchestrated under Kubernetes or OpenShift
  • RAG orchestration providing contextual retrieval with source citation
  • A contractual web portal for drafting, validation workflow, versioning and dashboards, consuming the AI farm through secured APIs behind firewall/WAF and load balancer
  • AI capabilities: assisted drafting from dynamic templates with parameterised clauses, semantic analysis and risk clause detection, compliance checking against internal standards and regulatory obligations, version comparison with impact qualification, obligation and deadline extraction, intelligent question-answering across the contract estate, and assisted regulatory monitoring
  • Governance by design: human-in-the-loop validation on every legal decision, full traceability of queries, sources and responses, explainability through cited extracts, role separation across lawyers, validators, administrators and auditors, and a hybrid AI-plus-business-rules approach for critical checks
  • Security architecture: network isolation, encryption, RBAC, complete interaction logging, and local retention of both data and models
  • A four-phase roadmap: scoping, document foundation, priority AI use cases, then extension to regulatory monitoring and risk mapping
Technologies
On-premises LLM servingDeepSeek / Llama / MistralRAG orchestrationVector databaseOCR pipelineKubernetes & OpenShiftGPU infrastructureSecured APIs
Outcome

A sovereign AI platform positioned as an augmented legal assistant: it accelerates contract review, improves consistency across the legal function, surfaces risk earlier and makes the contract estate searchable in natural language — while contracts, models and inference all remain inside the operator's own infrastructure, and legal judgement stays with the lawyers.

Telecommunications · National incumbent operator

Digital commerce and self-care for a national telecom operator

ClientThe incumbent telecom operator in its market
ScaleSeveral million fixed and mobile subscribers
ChannelsPublic B2C portal, B2B portal, corporate site, mobile app
StatusIn production; headless commerce platform in delivery
Context

The operator serves several million subscribers across fixed and mobile telephony and plays a central role in national internet penetration. Its public digital channels are the primary self-service route for both consumer and business customers.

Challenge

Customers needed to manage their accounts, consult and pay invoices, track consumption and subscribe to offers online, across consumer and enterprise segments — while the corporate site had to remain a content publishing platform for a national audience. Availability and performance expectations were those of a national operator.

Solution
  • Public B2C self-care platform: account management, consumption tracking, offer and plan subscription, invoice consultation and online bank-card payment, including grouped bill payment
  • Dedicated B2B portal for enterprise customers with their own service and billing journeys
  • Corporate content publishing site for news, offers and institutional communication
  • Consumer Value Management services and targeted offer delivery
  • A centralised microservice API layer serving all channels
  • A new headless e-commerce platform in delivery: a decoupled commerce backend serving web and mobile front ends independently, with API gateway, centralised authentication and container-based deployment
  • Continuous corrective and evolutionary maintenance under SLA
Technologies
Headless e-commerce.NET CoreAngularFlutterAPI gatewayOAuth / identity federationOpenShift & KubernetesSQL Server
Outcome

Consumer and business customers manage their accounts, pay invoices and subscribe to offers through the operator's own digital channels, with a headless architecture now being introduced so web and mobile commerce experiences can evolve independently of the commerce backend.
