Who we are

We use AI. We build AI.

Both halves of that sentence matter. Every consultant here works with AI assistance daily — code generation and review, test scaffolding, migration scripting, documentation, data analysis. And the agent, RAG and document-intelligence systems we design for clients are the same patterns we run ourselves.

AI in how we deliver

AI-assisted engineering compresses the distance between a requirement and a working increment. More of your project's hours go into architecture, business logic and the decisions that need human judgement — fewer into boilerplate.

AI in what we build

Enterprise RAG, Copilot and custom agents, MCP tool integration, OCR and document intelligence — deployed on Azure OpenAI, or on sovereign on-premises models where your data cannot leave your infrastructure.

Practitioner credibility

We do not sell AI capability we have not operated. When we recommend a retrieval strategy, a guardrail design or a GPU sizing, it comes from having built and run it — including in fully sovereign, on-premises environments.
