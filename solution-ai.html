Enterprise AI

Your data. Your AI. Your control.

Move from AI experimentation to agents and retrieval systems that work with your organisation's real information, permissions and processes — on the cloud or entirely on your own infrastructure.

What we do

Most AI pilots stall for the same reason: the model is impressive, but it cannot see the company's actual information — or worse, it sees information the user should not have access to.

We build the layer in between. Connectors to where your content actually lives, document understanding that turns scanned and unstructured files into usable data, permission-aware retrieval that respects your existing access model, and agents that take action inside real business processes.

Where an agent needs to reach beyond documents — into an ERP, a billing platform, a ticketing system — we expose those systems as governed tools through Model Context Protocol servers, so integration is reusable and auditable rather than hard-wired per use case.

And where the data cannot leave your infrastructure, we deploy the whole stack locally: open-weight models served on your own GPU hardware, with RAG orchestration, vector database and API layer inside your network boundary.

Our AI engineering stack

What we actually build

Four capability layers. Most engagements combine two or three of them.

Enterprise RAG

Retrieval-augmented generation over your real content estate: chunking and embedding strategies tuned to document type, hybrid semantic and keyword search, re-ranking, citation of sources, and permission-aware retrieval so every answer respects the user's existing access rights.

AI agents

Agents that do more than answer: they call business systems, run multi-step reasoning, execute approved actions and hand off to a human when confidence is low. Built with Copilot Studio, the Microsoft 365 agent model, or custom orchestration where the workflow demands it.

MCP & tool integration

Model Context Protocol servers exposing your line-of-business systems, databases and APIs as governed, reusable tools — so an agent can query an ERP, a billing platform or a document repository through one consistent, auditable interface instead of bespoke glue per integration.

Document intelligence

OCR pipelines, automatic document classification, metadata and field extraction, and content understanding that turns scanned contracts and unstructured files into structured, queryable business data.

Deployment models

Cloud AI, sovereign on-premises, or hybrid

The architecture is the same. Where the model runs, and where your data sits, is your decision — and we have delivered both.

Cloud AIAzure OpenAI Service and Microsoft 365 Copilot, with your data governed inside your Microsoft tenant. Fastest route to production, lowest infrastructure burden.
Sovereign on-premisesOpen-weight models — DeepSeek, Llama, Mistral — served locally on your own GPU infrastructure, orchestrated under Kubernetes or OpenShift. Content, models and inference never leave your boundary.
HybridSensitive corpora processed on local models, general workloads on cloud AI, under one retrieval and orchestration layer with policy-based routing.

Sovereignty is a real capability here, not a slide. We have designed a fully on-premises AI platform — local LLM serving, RAG orchestration, vector database and secured API layer on dedicated GPU servers under Kubernetes or OpenShift — for a national operator handling legally sensitive contract content. If your regulator, your board or your sector requires that data and inference stay inside your infrastructure, that is an architecture we have already designed and specified.

Use cases

Where this applies

Employee knowledge assistant

Staff ask questions in natural language and get answers grounded in policies, procedures and contracts — limited to what they are entitled to see, with sources cited.

Contract and document review

Automated pre-validation review: risk clause detection, inconsistency identification, comparison between versions, and extraction of obligations and deadlines.

Operational agents

Agents that query business systems through MCP tools, draft responses, prepare approvals and escalate to a human when confidence is low.

Governance

Guardrails are part of the build

AI that cannot be audited will not survive contact with a regulated business. These principles are designed in, not retrofitted.

Human in the loop

No consequential decision executes automatically. The AI accelerates the work; the professional keeps the judgement and the accountability.

Full traceability

Every query, the sources retrieved, the response produced and any action executed are logged and reviewable.

Explainability

Answers cite the extracts and references that justify them, so a user can verify rather than trust blindly.

Granular access control

Roles separated across users, validators, administrators and auditors — and retrieval that never returns content the user could not open directly.

Hybrid AI plus rules

Critical checks run through deterministic business rules alongside the model, reducing exposure to hallucination where it matters most.

Data quality governance

Templates, reference corpora and source repositories are governed — because retrieval quality is bounded by the quality of what it retrieves from.
