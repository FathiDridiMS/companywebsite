Custom Apps & Commerce

If you can describe it, we can build it.

Custom applications, startup products, public-facing platforms, digital commerce and internal tools, with any integration, on cloud, on-premises or hybrid infrastructure. Boosted by AI, built to market best practices.

What we do

We have built national-scale customer-facing platforms: B2C and B2B self-care portals handling billing, payment and offer subscription for millions of subscribers, a headless e-commerce backend serving both web and mobile front ends, public licensing e-services for a government ministry, and internal benefit and voucher platforms.

What these projects share: critical processes built properly, at a scale and availability level where failure is visible to the whole market.

Our developers build boosted by AI, following market best practice as the baseline: clean architecture, API-first design, automated testing, CI/CD, secure coding, observability and documentation delivered with the code.

Microsoft technologies are a major part of our engineering capability. We also work in Angular, React, Flutter, Python and Rust, and deploy to Kubernetes and OpenShift where the platform requires it.

Our engineering model

Boosted by AI. Built to market best practice.

Our developers build with AI assistance as standard, code generation and review, test scaffolding, refactoring, documentation. The result is more delivered capability per sprint, and more of the team's attention on architecture and business logic.

Every build follows market best practice as the baseline: clean architecture and separation of concerns, API-first design, automated testing, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, secure coding and identity integration, observability, and documentation produced alongside the code rather than after it.
