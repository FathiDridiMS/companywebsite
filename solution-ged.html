Document Management

From documents to actionable intelligence.

A document management platform fully integrated with AI: capture, OCR, automatic classification, extraction, natural-language search, controlled workflow, audit trail and archival.

What we do

Organisations rarely have a document problem. They have a findability, control and traceability problem that shows up as a document problem — and AI is what finally closes it.

Our GED solutions centralise, organise and secure enterprise documents in a platform integrated with Microsoft 365, with AI-driven classification, extraction and semantic search on top of proper access management and full traceability.

We have deployed this at national scale, digitalising subscriber contracts across an entire commercial agency network, at executive level for the records of a multilateral institution, and as a sovereign on-premises AI platform for legal contract management.

The intelligence layer can run on cloud AI services such as Azure OpenAI, or on open-weight models like DeepSeek hosted on your own infrastructure — the platform design does not change, only where inference happens.

AI-integrated by design

Not a repository with AI bolted on

Our document management solutions are fully integrated with AI. Classification, extraction, search and review are AI-driven from the outset — and you choose where the intelligence runs.

AI capture & classification

OCR on scanned and photographed documents, automatic classification by document type, and extraction of the fields that matter — parties, dates, amounts, obligations, reference numbers.

Natural-language search

Ask a question of the repository instead of guessing keywords. Semantic retrieval finds the relevant contracts, clauses or records and cites the source document and passage.

AI-assisted review

Risk clause detection, inconsistency flagging, comparison between document versions, obligation and deadline extraction, and summaries of what changed — with human validation retained.

Where the AI runs

Cloud AI, or sovereign on-premises models

The same document platform, two intelligence options. The decision is driven by your regulatory position and whether you have — or plan to have — local infrastructure.

Cloud AI

Azure OpenAI Service and Microsoft AI services, operating on content governed inside your Microsoft 365 tenant. Fastest path to production, no GPU infrastructure to procure or operate, and full integration with Purview retention, sensitivity labels and DLP.

Best when your data classification permits cloud processing and you want capability quickly.

Sovereign on-premises AI

Open-weight models — DeepSeek, Llama, Mistral — served on your own GPU infrastructure, with the vector database, RAG orchestration and APIs inside your network. Neither the documents nor the inference leave your boundary, and the models remain under your control.

Best when you hold legally sensitive, regulatory or classified content, or already operate a local AI farm.

Designed and specified, not theoretical. We designed an on-premises AI layer over an existing enterprise document repository for a national telecom operator's legal function — local LLM serving, RAG orchestration, vector indexing and ingestion connectors reading from the existing GED — specifically so that contracts and regulatory content stayed within the client's own infrastructure.
