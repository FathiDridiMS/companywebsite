What licence management with us looks like

Advisory & optimisation

Review of current entitlements against actual usage and planned projects. Where you are over-licensed we say so; where a capability gap is blocking a project, we identify the minimum SKU that closes it.

Procurement

Supply of Microsoft licences with a single point of contact, local invoicing and documentation suitable for procurement and tender processes. We can also purchase licences on your behalf and invoice them within a project where that is simpler.

Lifecycle management

Renewal tracking, assignment and reclamation of unused seats, true-up preparation, and alignment of licence changes with your roadmap rather than with a renewal date that arrives by surprise.

Tenant & licence hygiene

Automated reporting on assigned versus active licences, orphaned assignments and duplicate entitlements, built on the same Microsoft Graph and PowerShell automation we use for provisioning.

Licensing for projects

Licence planning built into migration, security and AI projects from the assessment stage, so the commercial model and the technical design are agreed together rather than sequentially.

Combined with services

Licensing pairs naturally with our Support Desk and Managed Services: one partner for the entitlement, the administration and the support, with a single monthly report covering all three.
