About Consultim-IT

A Microsoft partner that stays after go-live.

Based in Tunisia, with an office in France. An independent SARL with no parent or subsidiary companies.

Who we are

Consultim-IT is a digital services company (ESN) specialised in digital transformation on Microsoft technologies. We accompany organisations across the full transformation lifecycle, from scoping and architecture through implementation and adoption to continuous evolution.

Our work spans enterprise AI and agent development, modern workplace environments, AI-powered document management, custom business application development and digital commerce, hybrid cloud modernisation and data governance, Microsoft cybersecurity, managed services and Microsoft licensing.

We operate across telecommunications, finance and insurance, industry, energy, the public sector, international organisations and agri-food, in Tunisia, France, Germany, Switzerland, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon and the Gulf.

Our values

  • Expertise: deep specialisation in Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Power Platform, Azure, AI and .NET
  • Flexibility: engagement models that fit how you want to work
  • Proximity: a shared working day, shared languages, and people you can reach
  • Innovation: AI and automation applied to real business processes

We accompany clients from requirements analysis and solution development, through integration, to evolutionary and corrective maintenance. That continuity gives us deep system knowledge, and it is what makes our support engagements work.

How we work in 2026

We use AI. We build AI.

Every consultant here works with AI assistance as part of their daily toolkit: code generation and review, test scaffolding, migration scripting, data analysis and documentation.

In practice, more project hours go into architecture, business logic and the decisions that need human judgement, and fewer into boilerplate. It also means the agent, RAG and MCP patterns we design for clients are patterns we run ourselves first, including fully sovereign, on-premises deployments where a client's data cannot leave their infrastructure.

Sprint reviews with you

Each sprint ends with a review where we present results and collect feedback, before the next sprint is planned.

A retrospective every sprint

Each sprint also closes with a retrospective evaluating practices and processes, so delivery keeps improving.

Regular coordination

Coordination sessions run throughout to track progress and surface roadblocks early, while teams work autonomously in between.

Q1

Do you only work with Microsoft?

No. Microsoft is our core area of expertise and we resell Microsoft licensing, but we are technology-open and integrate other technologies where they better fit the business problem and target architecture, including open-weight AI models deployed on client infrastructure.

Q2

Are you a software company, a consultancy or a managed service provider?

All three. We combine Microsoft platform expertise with software engineering, AI, cybersecurity, managed services and licensing, which is what allows us to take responsibility for a whole problem rather than one layer of it.

Q3

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. We complement internal teams through team extension, dedicated teams or full project delivery, depending on what you need.

Q4

Where are your teams based and in which time zones do you work?

Tunisia, with an office in France. Tunisia is in Central European Time, giving a full working-day overlap with European and Gulf clients.

Q5

What languages do you deliver in?

French, English and Arabic, including Arabic-capable trainers for end-user enablement.

Q6

Do you respond to public tenders and RFPs?

Yes, with direct experience of development-bank, ministry and state-operator procurement processes. Use the RFP contact route and we will confirm receipt with the administrative documents you need.

Q7

Can you provide client references?

Yes. We describe references publicly by sector and scale, and provide named references, detailed documentation and referee contacts under NDA or within tender responses where the client has authorised it.

Q8

What happens after go-live?

Evolutionary and corrective maintenance under a support contract, with defined response and resolution commitments, patch management, health checks and quarterly service reviews.
